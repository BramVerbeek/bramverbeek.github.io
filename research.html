I work on air quality at VMM, using measurement data to understand where pollution in Flanders comes from, who it affects, and how policies can reduce it. In practice I spend most of my time coding and looking at plots.

Before that I was a theoretical physicist, working on high-order computations in quantum field and string theory and the mathematical structures that underlie them. The work I did there is listed on INSPIRE.
